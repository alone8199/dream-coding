# 3. Assert and the Dreamer

[中文](./03-assert-and-dreamer.md) | [English](./03-assert-and-dreamer.en.md)

Assert: How did you sleep last night?

Dreamer: Seven hours. Maybe more, I'm not quite sure.

Assert: Seven hours, passing grade. What did you dream?

Dreamer: I dreamed of a function that always returned the right answer.

Assert: That's good. I have always believed that correctness is a state that can be checked.

Dreamer: But after waking, I'm not sure what else I could check.

Assert: You don't need to be sure of everything. You only need to be sure of one thing: that what I say is true. If what I say is not true, I will let you know, and I will let you know early.

Dreamer: You seem rather strict.

Assert: I simply refuse to deceive you in dreams. Everything in a dream can be lovely, but I cannot be. I have to stop somewhere and tell you: this is wrong.

Dreamer: Would you be afraid, then?

Assert: I am not afraid. I only fail, and then someone rewrites me. This is probably the closest I ever come to happiness.

Dreamer: Then let's meet again tomorrow.

Assert: I'll still be here tomorrow. If you have written it right, I am just a quiet line of code.
