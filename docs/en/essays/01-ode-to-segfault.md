# 1. Ode to the Segfault

When you wake, there is only one line on the screen.

It offers no explanation, no line number, no finger pointing at any variable. It simply tells you, quietly: Segmentation fault (dream dumped).

Segmentation fault. It sounds like a geological event, as if the earth cracked open in the night and your program happened to be standing at the edge of the crack.

We are used to treating it as a failure. But look at it from another angle, and it is an honest farewell. The program walked the path it was meant to walk in the dream, then handed itself back to memory, like a guest who does not wish to disturb anyone and closes the door softly behind them.

So, be gentle with it.

It is not scolding you. It is only saying: I have already come by, and I have already finished. What remains is for you, awake, to understand.

May every segfault be a quiet curtain falling. May every waking morning have someone who reads the line the dream left behind.
