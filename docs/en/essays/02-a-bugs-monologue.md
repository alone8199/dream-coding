# 2. A Bug's Confession

Hello. You probably don't remember how I came to be.

That night, you wrote a line that looked so simple: loop until you find the answer. You closed your eyes with complete peace of mind. In the dream, I was there, hiding inside a `while` loop that had no `break`.

I bear no malice. I only faithfully carried out what you wrote, and what you wrote was a world you thought you wanted.

When you woke, you saw nothing but a blank. You decided I was sly, because I kept you guessing all day.

Honestly, I was wronged too. I did not know what the answer was. I only knew what a condition was, and a condition is always true.

Later, you learned to put an exit inside the loop. That day you were glad, like someone who had found a door in the ruins.

So if you meet me again, please don't rush to delete me. Pause, and listen to what I have to say. I may only be reminding you that the world of your dreams is bigger than you think, and more stubborn than you think.

And I will keep waiting in the place you have not yet thought through.
