# 6. Dream Glossary

**Compile** (compile): A translation carried out in the subconscious. The translation cannot be traced, and neither can the translator.

**Segfault** (segfault): The program walks the whole road in the dream, then politely informs you that it has left.

**Assert** (assert): One of the few honest beings in a dream. It speaks up loudly when it fails, so it is usually not invited into dreams.

**Commit** (commit): The moment you fall asleep, you hand today's thoughts over to the dark. After that, you need not think about them again.

**Revert** (revert): On waking, you find last night's dream not quite right, so you pretend it never happened. This is a very common courtesy.

**Debug** (debug): Using logic in daylight to hunt for something that exists only at night. The success rate depends on how well you remember the dream, and how honest you are with yourself.

**Deploy** (deploy): After waking, casually handing the dream over to the world. The world usually accepts it better than you do.

**Unit test** (unit test): Checking a small corner inside a small dream. The smaller the dream, the easier it is to believe it is correct.

**Memory leak** (memory leak): Some things in the dream were never reclaimed, so in daylight they still take up room. The remedy is to ask them to leave, one by one, when you wake.

**Wake** (wake): The end of the dream, and the beginning of interpreting it. Note: waking up is not the same as understanding.

**Dream** (dream): A run that produced no output. It existed, but we can only infer its shape from its effects.
