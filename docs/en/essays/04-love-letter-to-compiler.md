# 4. Love Letter to the Compiler

Dear Compiler,

Every day you do a very hard thing on our behalf. The sentences we write are vague and jumpy, carrying half-finished thoughts, and you take them apart one by one, checking every bracket, every quotation mark, every premise we never said aloud.

Sometimes you refuse me. You say the third line is missing something; you say you can't make sense of this symbol. I know, and back then I was angry. But looking back, I think you were only more honest than I was.

You never pretend to understand what I failed to write. You see only what I actually wrote.

I wrote many sentences in my dreams, and most of them I forgot on waking. But I have always remembered your error messages. They are like postcards, each one reminding me that between what I mean and the words I write, there is still a long road.

So, thank you. Thank you for guarding those characters while I slept, and thank you for pointing out, before I woke, every mistake you could find.

You need not love me. As long as you stay honest, that is enough.

With love,
One who revises dreams by day
